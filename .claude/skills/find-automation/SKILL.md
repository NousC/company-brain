# Find automation

1. **Look at what repeats** in this person's week: `tasks`, `attention`, and what they keep asking you for. Ask them too.
2. **Pick one.** Choose the job that happens most and needs the least judgment. Name it in a sentence, with how often it happens.
3. **Check it isn't already covered,** with `rev_plays` and `my_desk`.
4. **Draft it** as a RevPlay with `rev_plays`: what starts it, the steps, and where the result goes. Anything a customer would see stays a draft for a person to send.
5. **Show the preview** and wait for a yes before saving.
6. **Save it as theirs.** If it could help the team, tell them to ask an operator to share it.
