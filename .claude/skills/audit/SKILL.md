# Audit

Reads only. Changes go through `rollout`.

1. **Read the plan:** `Strategy/`, active `Initiatives/` and their success criteria.
2. **Check each criterion against real data:** deals, tasks, `attention`, `get_insights`. Say met, not met, or can't tell. A status field is a claim, not proof.
3. **Check the team:** who has no agent connected, who has gone quiet, and who's missing the skills their role needs, from Settings → Team and `assign`.
4. **Report the few things that matter,** each with its evidence and one next step. Say plainly what you couldn't check. You can see that an agent ran, not whether a person acted on it.
