# meeting-prep

_Prepare for a call. Use when someone mentions an upcoming meeting or asks for prep._

Call `context` with `intent: "meeting_prep"`, then read any playbook from `search` that fits this meeting.

Give them who's in the room, the last conversation, what's still open, and the one thing to get from this call. Name the objection they're likely to hear and the answer that has worked, from the objection playbook in `search`.

One screen. No summary of what they already know.
