# my-day

_What to do today. Use when someone asks what's on today or starts their day._

Call `my_desk`, then `priorities` and `tasks` for today.

Lead with the single most important thing: name the person, the company and the risk. Then, only if they earn it: promises coming due, deals going quiet, and the one thing to remember for each meeting today.

Two to five items, never more. If it's a quiet day, say so in one line.
